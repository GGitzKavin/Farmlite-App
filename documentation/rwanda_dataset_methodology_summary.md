# Rwanda Dairy Dataset Methodology Summary

## Design

- Cross-sectional; 66 lowland and 30 highland smallholder farms were purposively selected for having at least one lactating dairy cow.
- The related publication reports 96 dairy cows from 96 smallholder farms.
- Observational, cross-sectional, and purposively selected at the final cow/farm stage.

## Measurements

- Body weight: Estimated using a tape measure before morning feeding.
- Feed: Feeds were bagged and weighed with a hanging balance before offering; leftovers were weighed the following morning.
- DMI: Daily DMI was calculated as dry matter served minus leftovers.
- Milk: Recorded using graduated plastic jars after each milking session; the repository describes reported results in litres per day.
- Water: Recorded from the number of graduated jerry cans provided to cows daily.
- Feed samples: Composite 250 g feed samples were analysed for DM, CP, NDF, and ME.
- Laboratory methods: DM: oven-dried at 60 C for three days; protein: Kjeldahl; NDF: ANKOM F57 fibre bags; ME: documented equation using gas volume and CP.

## Calculations

- Feed characterisation used FarmDESIGN methods and cow production requirements used LIGAPS dairy models/equations.
- Milk, DMI, water, ME, and CP gaps are calculated, not directly measured.
- Potential milk and nutrient requirements are model/equation derived.

## Audit Caveats

- Twenty-eight negative leftover values conflict with physical next-morning weighing.
- The highland age column contains breed descriptions.
- NDF unit metadata conflicts with value semantics.
- CP-gap metadata wording conflicts with repository equations and values.
