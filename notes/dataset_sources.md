## Provenance status

The repository does not currently contain an authoritative source URL,
publisher, license, collection protocol, or data dictionary for either file.
The regular schemas and sequential identifiers may indicate synthetic or
generated data, but that has not been proven.

Before using these datasets for genuine dairy-cattle feeding decisions, record:

- the original publisher and download URL;
- the license and allowed uses;
- cattle population, geography, breed coverage, and collection dates;
- measurement and label definitions;
- missing-data and quality-control procedures;
- veterinary or animal-nutrition validation;
- known synthetic or generated fields.

Until that information is available, use the datasets only for exploratory or
educational development and avoid real-world nutrition claims.
